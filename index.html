Methodology and sources

Five indicators

  1. Business taxation: combined statutory corporate income tax rate, including central and sub-central rates.
  2. Public expenditure: general government total expenditure as a percentage of GDP.
  3. Public employment: general government employment as a share of total employment.
  4. Public personnel costs: general government compensation of employees as a percentage of GDP.
  5. Product Market Regulation: OECD PMR indicator, measuring regulatory barriers to competition.

Data sources

  • OECD – Corporate Tax Statistics
  • Eurostat – Government expenditure and personnel costs
  • OECD – Public employment
  • OECD – Product Market Regulation

The indicators measure different dimensions of the state's economic footprint. Public expenditure and public employment do not, by themselves, measure the quality of public services. Years and definitions must be checked before calculating a composite score.
