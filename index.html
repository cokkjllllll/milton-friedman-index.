Methodology and sources

How the index works

The index compares five dimensions: business taxation, public expenditure as a share of GDP, public employment, government personnel costs as a share of GDP, and OECD Product Market Regulation.

Each indicator will be normalised before aggregation. The methodology will specify the direction of each measure, weighting, reference year and treatment of missing data.

Traffic-light system

Green Lower measured State burden.

Yellow Intermediate measured burden.

Red Higher measured burden.

Grey means data are not yet verified. No colour is assigned in advance.

Sources

Eurostat · OECD · European Commission · National statistical authorities.

Every published value will link to its original source and identify the reference year.

Important distinction

Public spending and public employment measure the size of government. They do not, by themselves, measure regulatory restrictions, public-service quality or economic outcomes.

Data integrity: This is a research framework, not a completed ranking. No country score or traffic-light result is published until the underlying figures and calculation rules have been checked.
