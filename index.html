European Milton Friedman Index

How much does the State weigh on business?

A comparative index of the economic weight of the State across ten European countries.

Measuring the weight of the State

The European Milton Friedman Index compares ten European economies through five indicators: business taxation, public expenditure, public employment, personnel costs and product market regulation.

The aim is to provide a clear, evidence-based comparison of the conditions under which businesses operate across Europe.

Research status
The framework is established. Data collection and source verification are in progress. No country ranking or final score is published until the indicators are complete and comparable.

Methodology

The index uses five indicators drawn from internationally comparable statistical sources, including Eurostat, the OECD and national statistical authorities. Each indicator will be accompanied by its reference year, definition and source.

Green, yellow and red classifications will be assigned only after the data have been verified and the calculation method has been published. The indicators measure different dimensions of the State's role and will not be treated as interchangeable.
